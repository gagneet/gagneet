StrataOS
Multi-tenant strata management platform running East Gate Residences under NSW/ACT strata law. Covers a double-entry general ledger, levy runs and arrears, AI OCR for supplier invoices, maintenance and work orders, AGM voting, compliance registers, and a resident portal with chat and marketplace. Every building is an isolated tenant enforced by PostgreSQL row-level security. Money is stored as integer cents. Records are soft-archived for 7-year retention. The system of record is moving from MongoDB to PostgreSQL through a shadow-read cutover that reconciles both stores against live data.

How I Ship

Testing, Security & Performance

Two decades of quality, performance and automation engineering shape how I build my own products. Nothing goes out untested, unguarded or unmeasured.

Careful Testing
  • 17,000+ automated tests across my live products: pytest, Jest, Vitest and Playwright
  • Tenant-isolation tests prove one building's data never leaks into another's
  • CI guardrails fail the build on any duplicate or divergent financial calculation
  • Figures reconciled against production data, not just against the code
Security by Design
  • PostgreSQL row-level security, TOTP MFA, CSRF protection and rate limiting
  • Signed, expiring links, honeypots, and an outbound email queue that holds mail for review
  • Dependency audits and security scans in CI, plus spreadsheet formula-injection guards
  • Audit trails, soft-archive retention, and uniform 404s so attackers can't enumerate records
Performance Engineering
  • k6 load and fan-out benchmarks with Grafana dashboards
  • Background job queues (ARQ, Redis, Temporal) keep slow work off request paths
  • Expensive analyses computed on demand and marked stale when inputs change
  • Self-hosted with nginx, PM2, systemd and Cloudflare Tunnel, monitored and tuned in production
